Engineering Excellence Index

Color-Coded Thresholds & Criteria

Green = On Target
Amber = Below Target
Red = Critical

🚀 Velocity — Sprint Commitment vs Delivery (15%)

📊 Formula: (Story Points Delivered / Story Points Committed) × 100

⏱️ Measurement: Averaged across releases

Score Criteria Delivery Ratio
On Target Delivers the majority of committed work with minor variance ≥85%
Below Target Regular shortfalls in delivery against commitment 70% – 84%
Critical Significant and recurring gap between commitment and delivery < 70%

✅ Quality — Escaped Defect Rate (30%)

📊 Formula: (Defects Found in UAT / Total Defects Found) × 100

⏱️ Measurement: Rolling average across the evaluation period

Score Criteria Escaped Defect Rate
On Target Most defects caught pre-release; occasional escapes are non-critical ≤ 10%
Below Target Notable volume of defects reaching production; review and test gaps evident 11% – 20%
Critical High rate of production defects indicating systemic quality issues > 20%

🛡️ Production Stability — Application Incidents (30%)

📊 Formula: Total P1-P2 production incidents where root cause is product/application code

⏱️ Measurement: Quarterly

Score Criteria Incidents / Quarter
On Target Occasional incidents with fast recovery and clear corrective actions 0 – 2
Below Target Recurring incidents suggesting gaps in release validation or testing 3 – 5
Critical Frequent incidents with material customer or business impact > 5

🔒 Security Index (15%)

📊 Tracks implementation of mandatory Runtime and SDLC security best practices

⏱️ Measurement: Quarterly | Threshold: 80%

Score Criteria Rating
On Target Compliant with security best practices and thresholds > 80%
Below Target Non-compliant with mandatory security standards < 80%

📈 Engineering Excellence Index Calculation

Index = (Velocity Score × 0.15) + (Quality Score × 0.30) + (Stability Score × 0.30) + (Security Score × 0.15)

Overall Rating

Rating Index Score Bonus Multiplier Payout
On Target > 80% 1.0x > 80% of target
Below Target 50% – 79% 0.5x 50% – 79% of target
Critical < 50% 0x < 50% of target
